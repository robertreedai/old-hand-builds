Dictated end-of-shift notes → clean pass-down

How to dictate

Say "next" between items. Everything between two nexts stays together as one item.

Optional: start an item with a section name (like "Dock" or "Safety") to send it there.

Dock, door 6 leveler stuck, tech on it. Next. Parts, ordered two photo eyes. Next.

More dictation tips

Say a section name (or one of its aliases from Setup) to start that section, then talk. Everything after goes there until you say another section name.

Say next or new item to start a new item. Everything between two nexts is one item, even if it runs several sentences. Example: Safety oil leak by door 4 next guard missing on the compressor. Tech is on it. Next. Parts need two belts.

No "next" at all? The bot splits on sentences and guesses the best section from keywords. Each item lands in exactly one section, and you can fix anything in Review.

Filler like and then we, um, uh, so yeah is trimmed from the start and end of items.

Runs 100% on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Notes and setup are saved only in this browser.

Tap a section tag to move an item. Tap the text to edit it. Split or delete below each item.

Sections

      
One section per line, in the order you want them. Optional aliases after a colon (words you might say to start that section). Optional extra keywords after a | help the bot guess when you don't say a section name. Lines starting with # are ignored.

      
Example: Lifts: lift, elevator | hydraulic, cable
