About & terms

Privacy. Handoff Bot has no accounts, no AI, no analytics, and no server of its own. What you type or dictate is saved only in this browser on this device, and the app does not upload it. Your phone's dictation or keyboard may use its own speech service. Copying or sharing sends your text wherever you choose. Our host, GitHub Pages, logs visitor IP addresses.

Accuracy. Handoff Bot only puts your words into the sections you choose. It does not check facts, catch mistakes, or understand what you mean. Dictation can mishear you, and "next" can split items in the wrong place. Read the whole passdown and fix it before you hand it off.

Safety section. The Safety section is just a shift note. It is not an incident report, OSHA record, or compliance record. It does not replace your employer's required safety reporting, lockout/tagout procedures, or recordkeeping. Report hazards, injuries, and near misses right away through your employer's official channels. In an emergency, call for help first.
